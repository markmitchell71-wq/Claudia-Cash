Build a payoff plan

Amount available starts from your current Safe-to-spend surplus; Cash to preserve starts from your Settings → Funding rules cushion — adjust either before building. Highest APR first minimizes total interest paid (it fully pays down one card before moving to the next). Spread across top APR cards splits the same money across your top 3 highest-APR cards at once — it trims more balances sooner but costs a little more in total interest, since none of them get fully knocked out as fast. Build both with the same amount and compare the numbers below.

Compare strategies

Simulates each strategy month-by-month at your current Amount available, held constant, with a paid-off card's minimum rolling into the next month's extra — then compares total interest against paying only minimums. Cards without an APR are assumed interest-free here; cards without a minimum are estimated at whichever is larger of $25 or 2% of balance. Treat this as a rough, same-terms comparison, not a guarantee.
