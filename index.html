Build a payoff plan

Amount available starts from your current Safe-to-spend surplus; Cash to preserve starts from your Settings → Funding rules cushion — adjust either before building. Highest APR first minimizes total interest paid (it fully pays down one card before moving to the next). Spread across top APR cards splits the same money across your top 3 highest-APR cards at once — it trims more balances sooner but costs a little more in total interest, since none of them get fully knocked out as fast. Build both with the same amount and compare the numbers below.

Compare strategies

Simulates each strategy month-by-month at your current Amount available, held constant, with a paid-off card's minimum rolling into the next month's extra — then compares total interest against paying only minimums. Cards without an APR are assumed interest-free here; cards without a minimum are estimated at whichever is larger of $25 or (1% of balance plus that month's interest). Treat this as a rough, same-terms comparison, not a guarantee.

Account & Sync

Monthly card payments

Once a month, set each credit card bill to what you actually paid on it the month before. You review every change first; unpaid amounts in this and future pay cycles follow along.

PayPal

Monarch records most PayPal purchases twice — on your PayPal account and again on the bank account or card PayPal charged. The app keeps the bank/card copy (that's where the money actually left) and skips the PayPal copy in subscriptions, spending, budgets and bill matching. Skipped rows still appear on the Transactions tab, labeled.

Automatic backups

A full copy of your data is saved on this device right before every CSV import. The last 3 are kept. Download one to keep it, or restore it to undo an import.

Merchant renames

Monarch sometimes shows a merchant under a name you don't recognize. Renames apply to every matching transaction now and on every future import. Bill matching still recognizes the original name.
