Import balances

Choose a Monarch Balances CSV
The most recent row for each account is used.
Expected columns: Date, Balance, Account. Accounts are matched by their “statement name”, and anything that doesn’t match a cash account is checked against your credit card names. Card balances are stored as positive amounts owed.

Import Monarch transactions

Choose a Monarch CSV export
Monthly or full-history CSV is fine. Existing duplicate rows are ignored.
Expected common columns include Date, Merchant, Category, Account, Amount, Notes. The importer is flexible about capitalization and also recognizes “Original Statement” / “Description” as merchant-like fields.
